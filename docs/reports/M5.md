## Judgment calls `[autonomy]`

- **An ended game belongs to its participants and the admin (HIST-02).** Room members who weren't in it, e.g. onlookers, lose access to it when it ends, including its summary. Only the game's players and DM are sent to the summary page.
- **Stats for anyone, games only for those who shared them (HIST-04).** Anyone signed in sees a profile's numbers. The list of games on a profile shows only the games the viewer may open, so it is complete on your own profile (and for the admin) and empty for strangers.
- **A win is judged by the final alignment; most-played roles use the starting role** (as decided in the rules). History rows show the starting role too.
- **Account deletion (HIST-06):** only the admin, never their own account, and not while the user sits in a game in progress. It removes the sign-in identity, email and nickname; the seats they held stay, shown as 已删除用户, with the roles they played.
- **Guest stats:** guests have none. When a guest links Google, all their earlier games count at once (STATS-07).
